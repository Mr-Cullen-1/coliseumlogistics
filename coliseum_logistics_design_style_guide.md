# Coliseum Logistics School — Website Design & Style Guide

## 1. Project Context

**Brand:** Coliseum Logistics School  
**Industry:** Education / USA Trucking & Logistics Training  
**Website type:** Single-page visit / lead-generation landing page  
**Primary goal:** Build trust, explain the training program clearly, and convert visitors into applicants or leads.

This is **not a trucking company website**.  
It is a **professional logistics education brand** that teaches people how to build a career in the U.S. logistics/trucking industry.

The final visual direction should feel like:

> **Premium American Logistics Academy**

The website must combine:
- Professional logistics / trucking industry credibility
- Modern education / career-training clarity
- Premium, serious and trustworthy branding
- Strong conversion-focused landing page structure

---

## 2. Core Design Direction

Use a visual mix inspired by:

- **Cargo+** — strong logistics layout, industrial confidence, large typography
- **Aptus** — education structure, course/program presentation, enrollment UX
- **Shipping TNC** — dark professional logistics atmosphere
- **DriveSchool TNC** — transport + education combination
- **Zaiedu** — clean modern landing-page spacing and hierarchy

Do **not** copy any template directly.

The result must feel custom and specific to **Coliseum Logistics School**.

---

## 3. Brand Personality

The website should feel:

- Professional
- Premium
- Modern
- Confident
- Serious
- Career-oriented
- American logistics inspired
- Practical rather than academic
- Trustworthy rather than flashy

Avoid:
- Generic online-course / EdTech SaaS look
- Cheap trucking-template appearance
- Corporate blue logistics clichés
- Excessive gradients
- Overly futuristic UI
- Crypto / casino / luxury-club aesthetics
- Too much gold
- Cartoon illustrations
- Overloaded layouts

---

## 4. Visual Language

### Overall mood

Dark, premium, industrial and clean.

Think:
- Dark office environment
- Dispatch workstations
- Logistics maps
- Trucking operations
- Real mentors
- Real students
- Screens / dashboards / routes
- U.S. trucking industry context

The website should visually communicate:

> “You are entering a serious professional training environment connected to the real U.S. logistics industry.”

---

## 5. Color System

### Primary Palette

| Purpose | Color |
|---|---|
| Main background | `#0D1114` |
| Secondary dark surface | `#171B1E` |
| Elevated cards / panels | `#1D2226` |
| Primary bronze | `#C19A63` |
| Light gold accent | `#E0C28C` |
| Warm ivory | `#F3EFE7` |
| Primary text | `#F5F3EE` |
| Secondary text | `#A7AAAD` |
| Muted text | `#73777A` |
| Border / divider | `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` |

### Usage rules

- Dark colors should dominate the website.
- Bronze/gold is an **accent**, not the main surface color.
- Use bronze for:
  - CTA highlights
  - icons
  - small labels
  - selected states
  - section accents
  - thin decorative lines
- Do not use bright yellow gold.
- Avoid glossy or metallic gradients unless extremely subtle.
- White/ivory areas may be used occasionally to create rhythm, but the overall identity should remain dark.

---

## 6. Typography

Use modern, clean sans-serif typography.

Preferred style:
- **Headings:** Manrope / Satoshi / Inter Tight / Geist
- **Body:** Inter / Geist / Manrope

### Typography character

Headlines:
- Large
- Strong
- Compact
- High contrast
- Minimal line count

Body:
- Easy to scan
- Maximum readable width
- Short paragraphs

### Example scale

Desktop:
- Hero H1: `64–80px`
- H2: `42–56px`
- H3: `26–32px`
- Body large: `18–20px`
- Body: `16px`
- Small text: `13–14px`

Mobile:
- Hero H1: `40–48px`
- H2: `30–36px`
- H3: `22–26px`
- Body: `15–16px`

Avoid decorative serif fonts for the main UI.  
The logo already provides the Roman / Coliseum identity, so the website typography should stay modern and clean.

---

## 7. Logo Usage

The official Coliseum Logistics logo will be provided separately.

When the logo is added:

- Keep its original proportions.
- Do not redraw or modify it.
- Do not change logo colors without explicit instruction.
- Use a clean safe area around it.
- Prefer the logo on dark backgrounds.
- Avoid oversized logo usage.
- The brand should not rely only on the logo; the whole UI must visually support it.

Until the logo is provided, use a simple placeholder:
`COLISEUM LOGISTICS`

Do not create a fake replacement logo.

---

## 8. Image Direction

Use **realistic professional photography**, not generic stock-business images.

Preferred imagery:

1. U.S. trucks on highways
2. Dispatch/logistics office environments
3. People working with multiple monitors
4. Mentor explaining logistics operations
5. Students learning in a real office setting
6. Maps, dispatch screens, routes, freight operations
7. Details of keyboards, monitors, route dashboards, logistics documents

### Important rule

Do **not** fill the page with truck images.

If trucks dominate the website, visitors may think this is a transportation company.

Recommended visual balance:

- 40% people / office / education
- 30% dispatch / screens / operations
- 20% trucks / highways
- 10% abstract detail / maps / textures

Photography should feel:
- Cinematic
- Professional
- Slightly desaturated
- Dark / neutral
- High-quality
- Authentic

---

## 9. Layout Style

Use a strong editorial grid.

Recommended:
- Max content width: `1200–1320px`
- Large horizontal spacing
- Strong vertical rhythm
- Sections with generous whitespace
- Cards with restrained radius
- Strong separation between content groups

### Border radius

Keep it modern but not overly soft.

Recommended:
- Buttons: `10–14px`
- Cards: `16–20px`
- Large media blocks: `20–28px`

Avoid excessive pill-shaped UI.

---

## 10. Buttons

### Primary CTA

Style:
- Bronze background
- Dark text
- Medium-bold label
- Clear hover feedback

Examples:
- `Apply for Training`
- `Start Your Career`
- `Enroll Now`

### Secondary CTA

Style:
- Transparent or dark surface
- Thin neutral border
- Light text

Examples:
- `Explore the Program`
- `See How It Works`

Do not overuse CTAs.  
Each section should have one clear action priority.

---

## 11. Hero Section Direction

The hero should immediately answer:

1. What is this?
2. Who is it for?
3. Why should I trust it?
4. What should I do next?

### Suggested structure

**Eyebrow:**  
`USA LOGISTICS CAREER TRAINING`

**Headline:**  
`START YOUR CAREER IN U.S. LOGISTICS`

**Supporting text:**  
`Learn logistics in 1 month from professionals with 7+ years of real industry experience.`

**Primary CTA:**  
`Apply for Training`

**Secondary CTA:**  
`Explore the Program`

### Trust line

Show concise proof points directly in or below the hero:

- `7+ Years in Logistics`
- `Real Office Training`
- `Expert Masterclasses`
- `Interview Preparation`

### Hero visual

Preferred options:
- Real dispatch office
- Mentor + student
- Large monitor with logistics map
- Dark image with subtle bronze UI elements

Avoid generic hero illustrations.

---

## 12. Recommended Landing Page Structure

### 01. Header
- Logo
- Program
- About
- What You Learn
- Reviews
- Contact
- Primary CTA

Header can be transparent over hero initially, then become solid/sticky on scroll.

### 02. Hero
Strong headline, short explanation, CTA, trust markers, powerful visual.

### 03. Social Proof / Quick Stats
Examples:
- `7+ Years Industry Experience`
- `1 Month Training`
- `Real Office Practice`
- `Interview Preparation`

Use large numbers and concise labels.

### 04. Why Coliseum Logistics
Explain why this training is different.

Focus on:
- Real industry experience
- Practical learning
- Actual U.S. logistics workflows
- Professional environment
- Career preparation

### 05. What You Will Learn
Use structured cards or modules.

Potential topics:
- U.S. trucking industry basics
- Dispatch workflow
- Load boards
- Communication with drivers and brokers
- Documentation
- Rate negotiation
- Real working scenarios
- Interview preparation

Do not invent final curriculum details unless provided by the client.

### 06. How Training Works
A clear step-by-step process.

Example:
1. Learn fundamentals
2. Practice real workflows
3. Join expert masterclasses
4. Prepare for interviews
5. Start applying

### 07. Real Office / Learning Environment
Photo-focused section.

Use real office images if available.

### 08. Mentor / Expertise
Show real people and their experience.

Keep it professional and credible.

### 09. Reviews / Feedback
Use real student feedback only.

The Instagram account currently has a **Feedback** highlight, so this section should visually connect with social proof.

### 10. FAQ
Short, practical questions.

### 11. Final CTA
Strong conversion section.

Example:
`Ready to Start Your Logistics Career?`

CTA:
`Apply for Training`

### 12. Footer
- Logo
- Social links
- Telegram
- Instagram
- Contact
- Location
- Legal / copyright

## 13. Card Design

Cards should be:
- Dark
- Clean
- Spacious
- Minimal
- Border-driven rather than shadow-heavy

Suggested style:

```css
background: #171B1E;
border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08);
border-radius: 18px;
```

Hover:
- Slight border brightening
- Very subtle translateY
- Optional bronze accent

Avoid glowing cards.

---

## 14. Icons

Preferred:
- Thin line icons
- Clean geometric style
- Single icon family

Use icons sparingly.

Bronze can be used for icon accents.

Do not use:
- Emoji inside website UI
- Multicolor icon sets
- 3D icons
- Cartoon icons

---

## 15. Background Details

Use subtle visual elements only:

- Fine grid
- Route/map lines
- Small geographic coordinates
- Subtle grain
- Thin bronze lines
- Soft radial lighting
- Monochrome logistics-map textures

These should stay in the background and never compete with content.

---

## 16. Motion & Interaction

Animations should feel premium and controlled.

Use:
- Fade + slight vertical movement
- Staggered card reveal
- Slow image scale on hover
- Small CTA arrow movement
- Smooth counters
- Sticky header transition

Avoid:
- Large bouncing animations
- Constant floating elements
- Excessive parallax
- Flashy 3D effects
- Long page-load animations

Animation principle:

> Motion should improve hierarchy and perceived quality, not become entertainment.

---

## 17. Responsive Design

The website must be designed mobile-first as well as desktop.

### Mobile priorities

- Strong hero within first screen
- CTA visible early
- No tiny text
- No horizontal overflow
- Cards stack vertically
- Images remain meaningful when cropped
- Navigation becomes clean menu
- Keep text sections short
- Maintain comfortable spacing

Do not simply shrink the desktop layout.

---

## 18. Accessibility & UX

Maintain:
- Strong text contrast
- Minimum readable font sizes
- Clear CTA states
- Keyboard-friendly navigation
- Visible focus states
- Logical heading hierarchy
- Correct button/link semantics

Avoid putting important text directly over visually noisy images without a dark overlay.

---

## 19. Important “Do Not” Rules

Do not create:
- A generic blue logistics site
- A standard trucking-company website
- An LMS dashboard style
- A university website
- A SaaS landing page
- A luxury black-and-gold casino aesthetic
- An overly decorative Roman/Coliseum themed website
- Excessive columns and tiny cards
- Huge blocks of copy
- Generic AI-generated illustrations
- Unnecessary gradients
- Glassmorphism everywhere

Do not use Roman columns, gladiators, statues or ancient-Rome visuals as the main design concept.

The **Coliseum** name should communicate strength and premium identity through the brand, not through literal Roman decoration.

---

## 20. Design Decision Rule

Whenever choosing between two design options, prefer the one that feels:

**more professional → more credible → cleaner → easier to understand → more connected to real logistics work**

over the one that is merely more decorative.

---

## 21. Final Creative Direction

The final website should visually sit between:

> **Premium logistics company × modern career academy**

but the content hierarchy must always make it clear that:

> **Coliseum Logistics is a school that prepares students for careers in the U.S. logistics industry.**

### Final keywords

`dark`  
`premium`  
`industrial`  
`career-focused`  
`modern`  
`credible`  
`practical`  
`American logistics`  
`real office`  
`bronze accent`  
`high contrast`  
`clean editorial layout`

---

## 22. Instructions for Claude

When implementing the website:

1. Follow this design system consistently.
2. Do not introduce new colors or visual styles without a strong reason.
3. Keep layouts clean and conversion-focused.
4. Maintain the same design language across all sections.
5. Prioritize real content and imagery over decorative UI.
6. Do not invent client claims, numbers, curriculum or testimonials.
7. Treat all unconfirmed business information as placeholder content.
8. Keep components reusable.
9. Ensure desktop, tablet and mobile layouts all feel intentionally designed.
10. When the official logo is supplied, integrate it without altering the mark.

Before making major visual changes, compare them against the core direction:

> **Premium American Logistics Academy**
